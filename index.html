Travel Guides & Commute Tips

Navigating Japan's Trains

Commute: Get a JR Pass before you arrive! It saves a lot of money if you are traveling between cities. Download the Navitime app to figure out which platforms to use.

Attractions: Book tickets for the Tokyo Skytree at least a week in advance online to skip the massive lines.
